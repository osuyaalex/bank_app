BankAL: data and account access

Last updated 7 September 2026

BankAL does not access your Google account, and it does not read your email.

An earlier version requested Google's gmail.readonly permission so it could look for transaction receipts in your inbox. That was removed. The app no longer signs in with Google, requests no Google permissions, and has no access to any Google service on your behalf.

What BankAL does instead

BankAL reads the transaction alerts your bank already sends to your phone by SMS, and turns them into a record of your spending. Those messages are read on your device, and their contents are never uploaded.

You sign in with an email address and a password. That is the only account the app has.

If you gave the old version access to Gmail

That access is no longer used or requested, and you can withdraw it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it will not affect BankAL.
