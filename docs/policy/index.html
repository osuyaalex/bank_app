BankAL Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

BankAL reads the transaction alerts your bank already sends you, so it can show you where your money goes. This page explains exactly what it reads, what stays on your phone, and the one thing that only leaves it if you ask.

SMS access and what we read

BankAL asks for permission to read your SMS messages. It looks only for bank transaction alerts — messages containing financial terms such as debit, credit, Amt or account — and from those it extracts:

  • the transaction amount
  • the merchant name or description
  • the transaction date
  • the account balance, where the message states one

Personal messages, one-time passwords and anything that is not a bank alert are ignored.

SMS messages are read and processed entirely on your device. Their contents are never uploaded, with one exception that only happens if you ask for it: if BankAL cannot understand your bank's alerts, you can offer to send us the layout of one, with every digit and every name removed on your device first. That is described in Reporting a bank we cannot read below.

Reporting a bank we cannot read

BankAL can only read a bank's alerts once it has been taught that bank's format. If yours is one it does not recognise, the app will offer to send us the shape of one of those messages so we can add support for it.

It is optional

Nothing is sent unless you tap Send this. You can use the app whether you help or not.

We show you exactly what would be sent, before you decide

Every digit is replaced with #, and every word that is not standard banking vocabulary is replaced with a placeholder — on your device, before anything is transmitted. A message like this:

Acct:2211234558
DT:23/08/2026 09:06:35 PM
NIP CR/MOB/ABUBAKAR ALIYU/PAL
DR Amt:300.00
Bal:142.92

is sent to us as this:

Acct:##########
DT:##/##/#### ##:##:## PM
NIP CR/MOB/<name> <name>/<w>
DR Amt:###.##
Bal:###.##

The app shows you this comparison in full before it asks.

After a report is sent, we ask separately whether you would like to be told when your bank is supported. It is optional, it is used for that and nothing else, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Withdrawing a report

Email us and we will delete any report linked to your account. Because reports carry your account identifier, we can find them.

How we store and share data

Your account and your spending records are stored in Firebase Firestore, operated by Google LLC, which acts as our storage provider. Format reports are stored there too.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.

How long we keep it

  • Authentication data — for as long as your account is active.
  • Spending records — the processed transaction summaries, for as long as your account is active.
  • Raw SMS messages — never stored and never transmitted. Only the processed summaries are kept, and — where you have explicitly chosen to send one — the redacted layout of an unreadable alert.
  • Format reports — until the bank format they describe is supported, and for up to twelve months afterwards so we can confirm the fix still holds.
  • Email addresses given with a report — deleted once we have written to you, or immediately on request.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored using Google's encryption protocols. Access is restricted to authorised personnel and secured by authentication controls.

Your controls

  • Revoke SMS access at any time in your device settings. BankAL will stop reading new alerts immediately.
  • Request deletion of your data from within the app, or by email.
  • Request deletion of any format report you have sent.
  • Decline to send a format report and continue using the app as normal.

Children

BankAL is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how BankAL handles your data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be brought to your attention in the app.
