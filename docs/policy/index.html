BankAL Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

BankAL reads the transaction alerts your bank already sends to your phone, and turns them into a picture of where your money goes. This page says exactly what it reads, what stays on your phone, what does not, and what you can do about any of it.

BankAL is operated from Lagos, Nigeria, by Alexander Osuya. Where Nigerian data-protection law applies, we are the data controller for the information described here.

The short version

Your bank messages are read on your phone and are never uploaded. What reaches our servers is the result: amounts, dates, and who you paid. Not the messages themselves.

There is one exception, and it only happens if you tap a button: if we cannot read your bank's format, you can send us the redacted layout of an alert. That is covered in Reporting a bank we cannot read.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, we run no analytics or tracking, and no artificial-intelligence service sees anything of yours.

What BankAL reads on your phone

Your bank alerts (SMS)

BankAL asks for permission to read SMS. It looks only for bank transaction alerts, meaning messages containing terms such as debit, credit, Amt, Bal or account, and from those it works out:

  • the amount
  • whether money went out or came in
  • the date and time
  • who was paid, or who paid you, as written in the message
  • the balance, where the message states one
  • which bank sent it

Personal messages, one-time passwords, delivery notifications and anything else are ignored. All of this happens on your device. The text of your messages is never uploaded, stored on our servers, or shown to anyone. What is saved is the result: a list of transactions.

It also reads in the background

So that new spending appears without you having to open the app, BankAL re-checks recent messages periodically while it is closed, and after your phone restarts. This is the same reading described above, done on your device, and it stops the moment you revoke SMS access.

Your location, once, to pick a currency

When BankAL first sets up your account it asks for location so it can show budgets in the right currency. It takes a single position reading, converts it to a country using your device's geocoding service, which is provided by Google on Android, and keeps only the resulting currency symbol.

Your coordinates are not stored and are never sent to us. If you decline, BankAL uses the Nigerian naira and everything else works normally.

Biometrics

If you switch on fingerprint or face unlock, that check is performed by Android on your device. BankAL is told only whether it succeeded. We never receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

What you give us

  • Your account. First name, last name and email address, with a password, handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see your password.
  • Pictures you choose to add. If you add an image to a budget category, it is uploaded to Firebase Storage and linked to your account.
  • Your budgets and corrections. The categories you create, the amounts you set, and the times you tell BankAL it filed something in the wrong place.
  • A notification token. An identifier from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you alerts about your own spending. It identifies the app on your device, not you personally.

Reporting a bank we cannot read

BankAL can only read a bank's alerts once it has been taught that bank's format. If yours is one it does not recognise, the app will offer to send us the shape of one of those messages so we can add support for it.

It is optional

Nothing is sent unless you tap Send this. You can use the app whether you help or not.

We show you exactly what would be sent, before you decide

Every digit is replaced with #, and every word that is not standard banking vocabulary is replaced with a placeholder, on your device, before anything is transmitted. A message like this:

Acct:2211234558
DT:23/08/2026 09:06:35 PM
NIP CR/MOB/ABUBAKAR ALIYU/PAL
DR Amt:300.00
Bal:142.92

is sent to us as this:

Acct:##########
DT:##/##/#### ##:##:## PM
NIP CR/MOB/<name> <name>/<w>
DR Amt:###.##
Bal:###.##

The app shows you this comparison in full before it asks.

Withdrawing a report

Ask us and we will delete any report linked to your account. Because reports carry your account identifier, we can find them.

Where your data is stored, and who else sees it

BankAL runs on Google Firebase, which processes data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication. Your sign-in details.
  • Cloud Firestore. Your transactions, budgets and categories.
  • Firebase Storage. Images you add.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging. Notifications.
  • Firebase App Check. Confirms requests come from the real app and not from something impersonating it.

Google stores this on servers that may be outside Nigeria. No one else receives your data. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for advertising, and we will disclose it only if the law requires us to.

What BankAL does not do

  • It does not read your email. BankAL once used Google's Gmail permission; it no longer does, and that access has been removed.
  • It does not send anything to an artificial-intelligence service. Earlier versions used one to categorise spending; it was removed because it performed no better than the app's own rules.
  • It runs no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind.
  • It does not connect to your bank, and it cannot move your money. It only reads messages your bank has already sent you.
  • It never uploads the text of your SMS messages.

How long we keep things

  • Your account and your records. Kept for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and they go with it.
  • The text of your SMS messages. Never stored at all, anywhere, at any point.
  • Format reports. Kept until the bank format they describe is supported, and up to twelve months afterwards so we can confirm the fix still holds.
  • An email address given with a report. Deleted once we have written to you, or sooner if you ask.
  • Your location. Not kept. Only the currency symbol it produced.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS and is stored using Google's encryption. Access rules on our database restrict every record to the account that owns it, and format reports cannot be read by the app at all, only written to. Access on our side is limited to the operator of the app.

Your rights and controls

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you may ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. You can also:

  • Revoke SMS access in your device settings at any time. BankAL stops reading new alerts immediately, and everything already saved stays yours to view or delete.
  • Revoke location access. The app keeps working.
  • Turn off notifications.
  • Delete individual transactions, categories, or your whole account, from inside the app.
  • Decline to send a format report, or ask us to delete one you sent.

Write to us using the details below and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

BankAL is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

This page is kept in the app's own source repository, so it changes in step with the app itself. If we change what BankAL collects, this page changes in the same release and the date at the top moves. Anything material will be brought to your attention inside the app.
